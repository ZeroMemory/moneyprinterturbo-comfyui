## LTX-2.5

For one take, write a chronological paragraph in the present tense. Give the
subject a concrete action, keep the lighting consistent and place pauses
where they should occur. A small gesture may need only a static close-up.

When the user requests a cut within the clip, name the transition in prose.
Establish the new angle and subject, repeat identifying details for returning
subjects and say which sounds continue or change. Keep requested dialogue in
quotation marks. Prefer an unbroken take when lip sync matters.
