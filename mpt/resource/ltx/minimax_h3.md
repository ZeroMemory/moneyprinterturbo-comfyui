## MiniMax H3

Write visual_prompt as one English string with three labeled sections in order.
Use the labels below inside the string.

integrated_multimodal_description: Begin with [Shot 1], the style and opening
composition. Describe the subject's movement, camera and sounds in playback
order. If a cut is requested, start [Shot 2] with its time in MM:SS.mmm and
describe the new view. Keep later cut times increasing and inside the clip.
For requested speech, identify each voice consistently as (S1), (S2) and so on.
Place delivery outside <d>[Language] exact words</d>. Preserve requested signs
or other visible writing in double quotes in their original language.

overall_soundscape: Describe ambience and physical sounds that belong in the
scene. Use N/A for an explicitly silent clip.

non_diegetic_music: Use N/A unless a score is requested. If it is, describe
instruments, pace and changes in volume.
