## LTX-2.3

Write each clip as one continuous take in a present-tense paragraph. Establish
the shot size, subject and light. Follow the action through to a visible
result. Give a longer take enough detail to fill it, such as a glance before
a turn or a hand settling after a gesture. Describe camera motion relative to
the subject and its final framing. Plan a new angle as a separate clip.

For requested speech, quote the line and place physical cues or pauses between
phrases without changing the words. Describe relevant sounds at their source.
